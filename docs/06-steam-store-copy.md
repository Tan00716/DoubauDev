## 2. Genre 定位

**英文版**

> **Minimalist RTS / Tower-Defense Deckbuilder**

EMBERHOLD sits at the intersection of three genres: real-time castle defense, deckbuilding, and roguelite progression. It is not a traditional tower defense where you watch towers fire, nor a card game where cards only exist in your hand. Your cards become permanent buildings and squads on the battlefield; your battlefield, in turn, becomes your hand during the night. The genre label that best captures this is **Minimalist RTS / Tower-Defense Deckbuilder**.

**中文版**

> **极简即时战略 / 塔防卡牌构筑**

《烬堡》横跨三种类型的交集：即时守城、卡牌构筑与 Roguelite 成长。它既不是传统塔防中「摆完看戏」的纯观赏型守城，也不是卡牌仅在手中流转的纯牌组游戏。你的卡牌会永久化为战场上的建筑与军团，而你的战场在夜间又反过来成为你打出的手牌。最能概括这一体验的 Genre 标签是：**极简即时战略 / 塔防卡牌构筑**。

## 4. Short Description（Steam About 一段版）

**英文版（约 280 词，适配 Steam About 一段）**

EMBERHOLD is a minimalist RTS where your castle is your deck.

By day, you spend gold to play cards onto the battlefield—walls, towers, barracks, markets, and squads of spearmen, archers, cavalry, and siege beasts. Each card becomes a permanent part of your fortress. By night, the enemy advances from multiple directions with visible threat previews, and your fortress transforms back into a hand of tactical cards: formations, elemental strikes, emergency reinforcements, and your commander's ultimate ability. You issue squad-level orders, time your plays, and hold the line until dawn.

Every surviving night deepens your build. Cards earn irreversible A/B branches. Squads gain veterancy. New territories expand your castle and shift your economy. But death is not the end—memory fragments unlock new commanders, new cards, new blueprints, and new enemy intel in the Kingdom Hall. There is no numerical meta-progression; your hundredth run starts with the same base stats as your first. The only thing that grows is your menu of options, and your understanding of how to combine them.

One run lasts 35–45 minutes across 6–8 day-night cycles. Ten distinct build archetypes, four rule-bending commanders, and a 200-card pool ensure no two castles look—or play—the same.

**中文版（约 360 字，与英文版语义对等）**

《烬堡》是一款极简即时战略游戏，你的城堡就是你的卡组。

白天，你消耗金币在战场上打出卡牌——城墙、箭塔、兵营、市集，以及枪卒、弓手、游骑与巨兽组成的军团。每张卡牌都会成为你城堡的永久组成部分。入夜，敌人从多路逼近，而你的城堡反过来化为一手战术牌：阵型切换、元素打击、应急增援，以及指挥官的终极技能。你以「班」为单位下达指令，把握出牌时机，守住防线直到天亮。

每一个存活的夜晚都会深化你的 Build。卡牌解锁不可逆的 A/B 分支，军团积累战勋，新领地扩张你的城堡并改变经济格局。但死亡并非终结——记忆碎片可在王国厅解锁新指挥官、新卡牌、新建筑蓝图与敌人情报。Meta 层零数值成长：你第 100 局的基础数值与第 1 局完全一致。唯一增长的是你的选项菜单，以及你对如何组合它们的理解。

一局持续 35–45 分钟，横跨 6–8 个昼夜循环。10 种截然不同的 Build 原型、4 名改写规则的指挥官、200 张卡牌池——没有两座城堡会长得一样，也没有两局的打法相同。

## 5. Full Store Description（完整商店页文案）

**英文版**

### About This Game

**Your castle is your deck.**

In EMBERHOLD, the cards you play during the day become the walls, towers, and armies that defend you at night. When darkness falls, those same buildings and squads transform back into a hand of tactical cards—formations, elemental strikes, reinforcements, and command abilities—that you must play wisely to survive until dawn.

This is not tower defense where you watch passively. This is not a card game where cards only exist in your hand. This is a **living fortress** that you design, build, and then command in real-time defense.

---

**Day: Build Without Pressure**

Spend gold to place cards on the map. Every wall segment, arrow tower, barracks, and squad you deploy is a permanent choice that shapes your defense. Position matters: archers on high ground gain +2 range. Shieldbearers backed against walls gain bonus block. Cavalry in forests gain stealth. A narrow bridge with spike traps becomes a kill corridor. Your spatial decisions *are* your deckbuilding decisions.

---

**Night: Defend With Your Hand**

Before each night begins, you see exactly which enemies are coming, from which directions, and in how many waves. There are no surprises—only preparation versus reality.

Combat is **semi-automatic**: your squads fight according to daytime formations and rules, while you intervene with three tools: squad orders (move, retreat, hold, focus fire), tactical cards drawn every 8 seconds from a 5-card hand, and your commander's once-per-night ultimate ability. Pause at any time to issue orders. The depth comes from timing and synergy, not APM.

---

**A Deck That Lives on the Battlefield**

EMBERHOLD solves the classic deckbuilder problem of drawing dead cards. Your core power—the Armory layer of walls, towers, and squads—never leaves the field. The Tactic layer (your 5-card hand) only handles temporary interventions: a fire barrel to ignite terrain, a frost arrow to freeze a river, a phalanx formation to halve incoming damage. Bad draws slow you down; they don't delete your army.

Cards that share a name upgrade the existing entity instead of cluttering your deck. Every card also has an irreversible A/B branch that changes its mechanics—not just numbers. Your arrow tower can become a piercing ballista or an area-burst rain of arrows. Your shieldbearers can become an immovable taunt wall or a shock-troop charge line. These choices define your build archetype for the entire run.

---

**Four Commanders, Ten Builds, Infinite Castles**

Each of the four commanders rewrites 1–2 core rules, creating entirely different viable build spaces:

- **Orn, the Wander-Bard**: First tactical card each night is free; every third card of the same element reduces that element's cost by 1. Built for combo chains and engine play.
- **Vera, the Ash-Duchess**: Buildings can relocate once per night for free; walls slowly self-repair. Built for fortress engineering and turtle strategies.
- **Karn, the Dragonslayer**: All melee gains armor penetration; **cannot build ranged towers**; +4 squad capacity. Built for brute-force melee legions and beast hordes.
- **Morgun, the Death-Taxer**: Fallen squads refund 50% of their cost as battle-will; can recruit undead squads that vanish at dawn. Built for sacrifice and attrition economies.

Ten build archetypes—from Arrow-Rain Domain to Scorched-Earth Inferno to Frost-Lock Control—each use different cards, units, towers, and terrain strategies. Each is explicitly countered by specific enemy types and boss rules, ensuring no single build dominates.

---

**Bosses That Rewrite the Rules**

Nine bosses don't just have more health—they change how the fight works:

- The **Siege Titan** is immune to all tower damage. Only your squads can hurt it.
- The **Tide-Mother** floods the lowlands every 30 seconds, extinguishing fire builds and forcing elevation play.
- The **Memory-Thief** steals your most expensive Armory card at the start of the fight; kill its phantom to reclaim and permanently empower it.
- The **Chronomancer** rewinds all health to 10 seconds ago every 20 seconds, punishing slow grinds and rewarding burst windows.
- The **Usurper** ignores your defenses and attempts a 30-second "coronation" channel on your keep. Interrupt it, or lose.

Bosses are visible before you choose your route, so your entire run is shaped by the question: *"Can my current build handle what waits at the end?"*

---

**Zero Numerical Meta-Progression**

Between runs, the Kingdom Hall unlocks new options: new commanders, new cards added to the reward pool, new building blueprints, and enemy intelligence that reveals previously hidden threats. Nothing gives +5% damage. Nothing gives +10% health. Your 100th run starts with the exact same base stats as your 1st. The only difference is the size of your toolkit—and your mastery in using it.

---

**Built for Replayability**

- **Run 1**: Learn the day-night rhythm, lose to the first lord, and discover your first build archetype.
- **Run 5**: Switch from Orn's combo engine to Vera's fortress turtle. Face curses and high-risk events for the first time.
- **Run 20**: Encounter rule-breaking bosses like the Chronomancer and the Usurper. Begin chasing build-completion achievements.
- **Run 50**: Full 200-card pool unlocked. Attempt self-imposed challenges (no towers, one-squad runs) with tracked achievements.
- **Run 100**: Master the Ashlands Law system—stack up to 6 rule modifiers like "Fog Night" (range -2) and "Famine" (tax income -30%) for self-directed difficulty that never inflates enemy HP.

One run is 35–45 minutes. The road to mastery is 80–120 hours. Every castle is different. Every night is a new hand.

**中文版**

### 关于这款游戏

**你的城堡就是你的卡组。**

在《烬堡》中，白天打出的卡牌会变成夜间守护你的城墙、箭塔与军团。当黑暗降临，这些建筑与军团又反过来化为一手战术牌——阵型切换、元素打击、应急增援与指挥官技能——你必须明智地出牌，撑到天亮。

这不是「摆完看戏」的塔防，也不是卡牌只在手中流转的纯牌组游戏。这是一座**会呼吸的城堡**：由你设计、建造，然后在即时战斗中亲自指挥。

---

**白天：无压力地建造**

消耗金币在地图上放置卡牌。每一段城墙、每一座箭塔、每一座兵营、每一个军团都是永久选择，塑造你的防御体系。位置至关重要：弓手在高地射程 +2；盾卫背墙格挡加成；游骑在森林中隐身；窄桥配尖刺陷阱就是一条杀戮走廊。你的空间决策*就是*你的构筑决策。

---

**夜间：用手牌守住一切**

每夜开始前，你能精确看到敌人从哪来、走哪路、分几波。没有意外——只有准备与现实的较量。

战斗采用**半自动操控**：军团按白天的阵型与规则自动接战，你以三种方式干预：班级指令（移动、撤退、驻守、集火）、每 8 秒从 5 张手牌中抽出的战术牌，以及指挥官每夜一次的终极技能。随时可暂停发令。深度来自时机把握与系统联动，而非手速。

---

**一套活在战场上的牌组**

《烬堡》解决了传统卡牌构筑中「抽到废牌」的经典问题。你的核心战力——军械册层的城墙、塔与军团——永远留在场上。战术层（5 张手牌）只负责临时干预：一桶火油点燃地形、一支霜箭冻结河面、一个龟甲阵减半承伤。烂牌拖慢节奏，但不会让你的军团消失。

同名卡牌打出后会升级场上已有实体，而非占用牌组空间。每张卡还有不可逆的 A/B 机制分支——不是纯数值变化。箭塔可以变成贯穿直线的弩炮，或范围齐射的箭雨塔；盾卫可以变成不动如山的嘲讽铁壁，或冲锋开团的盾冲击线。这些选择定义了你整局的 Build 方向。

---

**四名指挥官，十种 Build，无数座城堡**

四名指挥官各改写 1–2 条基础规则，创造出截然不同的可行 Build 空间：

- **流浪战诗人·奥恩**：每夜第一张战术牌免费；每打出第三张同系别牌，该系别本夜费用 -1。适合卡牌连击与引擎流。
- **灰烬女爵·薇拉**：建筑每夜可免费位移一次；城墙夜间缓慢自愈。适合工事建造与龟城流。
- **屠龙者·卡恩**：全军近战附带破甲；**不能建造任何远程塔**；军令 +4。适合近战军团与巨兽流。
- **死灵税吏·莫尔甘**：班阵亡返还 50% 费用（战意形式）；可招募夜末消散的亡灵班。适合牺牲消耗与亡者归还流。

十种 Build 原型——从箭雨领域到燎原火海，从霜锁控场到战争经济——各自使用不同的卡牌、单位、塔与地形策略。每种都被特定敌人类型与 Boss 规则明确克制，确保不存在唯一最优解。

---

**改写规则的 Boss**

九个 Boss 不靠血量取胜——它们改变战斗规则本身：

- **碎城者**：免疫所有塔伤害，只有军团能对其造成伤害。
- **潮汐之母**：每 30 秒淹没低地，熄灭火焰 Build，强制高地作战。
- **盗忆贼**：开战时偷走你最贵的军械册卡；击杀其幻影可夺回并永久强化该卡。
- **时砂守望者**：每 20 秒回溯所有生命值至 10 秒前，惩罚慢磨，奖励爆发窗口。
- **王座篡夺者**：无视防御直奔主堡，30 秒「登基读条」——打断它，否则失败。

Boss 在选路线时即已可见，所以你整局的构筑都围绕一个问题展开：*「我当前的 Build 能否应对终局等待我的那个东西？」*

---

**Meta 零数值成长**

局与局之间，王国厅解锁新选项：新指挥官、新卡牌进入奖励池、新建筑蓝图、敌人情报揭示先前隐藏的威胁。没有任何 +5% 攻击。没有任何 +10% 生命。你第 100 局的基础数值与第 1 局完全一致。唯一的区别是你的工具箱大小——以及你使用它的熟练度。

---

**为重复游玩而生**

- **第 1 局**：学会昼夜节奏，输给第一位领主，发现自己的第一个 Build 原型。
- **第 5 局**：从奥恩的连击引擎切换到薇拉的龟城工事。首次面对诅咒与高风险事件。
- **第 20 局**：遭遇时砂守望者、王座篡夺者等规则级 Boss。开始追求 Build 完成度成就。
- **第 50 局**：200 张全卡池解锁。尝试自约束挑战（不用塔通关、单班通关）并追踪成就。
- **第 100 局**：掌握荒土法则系统——自选叠加最多 6 条规则修正，如「雾夜」（射程 -2）、「荒年」（税入 -30%）。难度来自规则复杂度，而非敌人血量膨胀。

一局 35–45 分钟。通往精通之路需要 80–120 小时。每座城堡都不一样。每个夜晚都是一手新牌。

## 7. Replayability Pitch（可重玩性卖点段落）

**英文版**

Every run of EMBERHOLD is a new castle, a new hand, and a new puzzle.

Your starting commander defines which rules you're playing under. Your chosen territory defines which enemies, terrain, and economy you'll face. Your first few card rewards set you on a build trajectory that the game's weighted reward system gently reinforces—while enemy types and boss rules actively challenge it. A/B branches are irreversible; a single early choice between "piercing ballista" and "rain of arrows" reshapes the next twenty cards you want. By the time you reach the final siege night, your fortress is a unique expression of every decision you've made.

Death is not a reset button—it's a lesson. The post-run analysis tells you exactly what killed you, which enemy type punished your build, and which gap in your defense was exploited. That knowledge feeds into the Kingdom Hall, where memory fragments unlock new tools for the next attempt. And because there is no numerical meta-progression, your next loss is never softened by invisible stats. You get better because you understand more, not because the game secretly made you stronger.

With 10 build archetypes, 4 commanders, 200 cards, 9 enemy types, 9 rule-bending bosses, 20 Ashlands Laws, and 8 territory templates, the number of meaningfully different castles you can build exceeds anything you'll exhaust in a hundred hours.

**中文版**

《烬堡》的每一局都是一座新城堡、一手新牌、一道新谜题。

你的初始指挥官定义了你在什么规则下游戏。你选择的领地决定了面对哪些敌人、什么地形、怎样的经济格局。前几夜的卡牌奖励将你推上一条 Build 轨道，而游戏的缺口加权奖励系统会温和地强化它——同时敌人类型与 Boss 规则会主动挑战它。A/B 分支不可逆；早期「弩炮」还是「箭雨」的一个选择，会重塑你接下来想要的二十张牌。当你抵达终局围攻之夜时，你的堡垒是你所有决策的独特表达。

死亡不是重置键——而是一堂课。结算页的死因分析会明确告诉你：什么杀死了你、哪种敌人惩罚了你的 Build、防御的哪个缺口被利用了。这些知识汇入王国厅，记忆碎片为下一次尝试解锁新工具。而因为 Meta 零数值成长，你的下一次失败不会被隐形的数值所软化。你变强是因为你理解得更多，而非游戏偷偷让你更强。

10 种 Build 原型、4 名指挥官、200 张卡牌、9 种敌人、9 个规则级 Boss、20 条荒土法则、8 张领地模板——你能建造出的真正有差异的城堡数量，远超一百小时内能够穷尽的极限。

## 8. Feature List（特性列表，Steam 页面 Bullet 形式）

**英文版**

- **Castle-Is-Deck Core Mechanic** — Play cards by day to build permanent walls, towers, and squads. At night, your castle transforms back into a hand of tactical cards you play to survive.
- **Dual-Layer Card System** — The Armory layer (units and buildings) stays on the field permanently; the Tactic layer (5-card hand) handles real-time interventions. Bad draws never delete your core army.
- **Semi-Automatic Combat with Pause** — Squads fight by daytime formation rules while you issue squad orders, play tactical cards, and deploy commander ultimates. Pause at any time to plan.
- **10 Distinct Build Archetypes** — From Arrow-Rain Domain to Scorched-Earth Inferno to Frost-Lock Control, each uses different cards, units, towers, and terrain strategies—and each is explicitly countered by specific enemies.
- **4 Rule-Bending Commanders** — Each commander rewrites core mechanics: no ranged towers, teleporting buildings, undead economies, or free combo chains. No numerical passives.
- **9 Bosses That Rewrite Combat Rules** — Immune to towers, flood the map, steal your cards, rewind time, or attempt a game-ending channel. Bosses are visible before you choose your route.
- **Zero Numerical Meta-Progression** — Unlock new cards, commanders, blueprints, and enemy intel between runs. Base stats never increase. Growth comes from options and mastery, not invisible bonuses.
- **Day-Night Dual Economy** — Gold (day/stockpile) vs. Battle-Will (night/flow). Hoarding gold raises upkeep; avoiding battle drains your ability to play cards. Forces constant engagement.
- **Weighted Reward System** — Card rewards are biased toward filling gaps in your current build, ensuring every run develops its own identity rather than converging on a single optimal deck.
- **Irreversible A/B Branches** — Every tower and squad upgrade is a binary mechanic choice, not a numerical bump. Your early choices reshape the entire run.
- **Threat Preview System** — See every wave's composition, route, and timing before night falls. No random ambushes. Preparation versus reality is the core tension.
- **35–45 Minute Runs** — 6–8 day-night cycles per run. A complete session that fits into an evening, with clear session boundaries and no mid-run save scumming.
- **80–120 Hours to Full Mastery** — 200-card pool, 20 Ashlands Law modifiers, build-completion tracking, self-imposed challenge achievements, and weekly seeded leaderboards.

**中文版**

- **城堡即卡组核心机制** — 白天出牌建造永久城墙、箭塔与军团；夜间城堡反过来化为一手战术牌，供你出牌求生。
- **双层牌库系统** — 军械册层（单位与建筑）永久留在战场；战术层（5 张手牌）负责即时干预。烂牌永远不会删除你的核心战力。
- **半自动战斗 + 全局暂停** — 军团按白天阵型规则自动接战，你下达班级指令、打出战术牌、释放指挥官终极技。随时可暂停规划。
- **10 种截然不同的 Build 原型** — 从箭雨领域到燎原火海，从霜锁控场到战争经济，每种使用不同的卡牌、单位、塔与地形策略——且每种都被特定敌人明确克制。
- **4 名改写规则的指挥官** — 每名指挥官改写核心机制：禁远程塔、建筑传送、亡灵经济、免费连击链。没有数值型被动。
- **9 个改写战斗规则的 Boss** — 免疫塔伤、淹没地图、偷走卡牌、回溯时间、或发动读条即失败的「登基」。Boss 在选路线时即已可见。
- **Meta 零数值成长** — 局间解锁新卡牌、指挥官、蓝图与敌人情报。基础数值永不增加。成长来自选项与精通，而非隐形加成。
- **昼夜双轨经济** — 金币（昼/存量）与战意（夜/流量）。囤金提升维持费，避战耗尽出牌资源。从机制上强制持续接敌。
- **缺口加权奖励系统** — 卡牌奖励按当前 Build 缺口加权出现，确保每局发展出独立身份，而非收敛到单一最优牌组。
- **不可逆 A/B 机制分支** — 每座塔、每个单位的升级都是二元机制选择，而非数值提升。早期选择重塑整局走向。
- **威胁预演系统** — 入夜前可见每波敌人的构成、路线与到达时间。没有随机伏击。准备与现实的较量是核心张力。
- **35–45 分钟一局** — 每局 6–8 个昼夜循环。适合晚间的一次完整体验，有清晰的对局边界，不支持夜中存档重读。
- **80–120 小时通往完全精通** — 200 张卡牌池、20 条荒土法则修正、Build 完成度追踪、自约束挑战成就、每周固定种子排行榜。
